BANKING POLICY

1. International wire transfer fee:
$25 per transaction.

2. Daily wire transfer limit:
$10,000 per customer.

3. Minimum checking account balance:
$500.

4. Savings account interest rate:
3.5% annually.

5. Fraud reporting:
Customers must report suspected fraudulent transactions
within 30 days.

6. Account closure:
Customers may request account closure through a branch
or authorized customer service representative.
